Our programs

Adoption

Every animal is health-checked, sterilised and vaccinated before going home. Our team matches animals to families.

Fostering

Foster carers give animals a calm home while they recover or wait for adoption. We supply food and veterinary care.

Sterilisation drives

We run low-cost community drives to reduce the number of unwanted litters.

Education

School visits teach children about responsible pet ownership and animal welfare.
