About Hope Haven

Our history

Hope Haven was founded in 2014 by a group of volunteers who turned a disused warehouse into a community shelter. Today we are supported by volunteers, sponsors and local businesses.

Mission and vision

Mission: To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned animals.

Vision: A community where every animal has a safe, loving home.
